# Recognition: Honors, achievements and privacy

[RecognitionServiceImpl](../../apps/api/src/main/java/me/nghlong3004/olympic/recognition/service/impl/RecognitionServiceImpl.java), [public controller](../../apps/api/src/main/java/me/nghlong3004/olympic/recognition/controller/RecognitionController.java), [admin controller](../../apps/api/src/main/java/me/nghlong3004/olympic/recognition/controller/AdminRecognitionController.java) and [requests/DTOs](../../apps/api/src/main/java/me/nghlong3004/olympic/recognition) own exact endpoints/fields. [Named features](../README.md#profile) and [identity/UI owners](../ui/owners/identity-and-recognition.md) connect routes, return navigation, upload and tests.

Honors albums are admin-edited memories with ordered photos and registered/historical attribution: drafts are admin-only, published albums are public, and albums never create achievements or award points. Academic achievements are separate evidence-backed submissions; only currently APPROVED records contribute points. Reapproval does not duplicate points; revocation removes them.

## Quy tắc điểm hiện có

| Nhóm | Nhất | Nhì | Ba | Khuyến khích | Tham gia |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Olympic toàn quốc (`OLYMPIC_NATIONAL`) | 10 | 9 | 8 | 7 | 6 |
| Olympic cấp trường (`OLYMPIC_SCHOOL`) | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| NCKH cấp Bộ (`RESEARCH_MINISTRY`) | 8 | 7 | 6 | 5 | Không áp dụng |
| NCKH cấp trường (`RESEARCH_SCHOOL`) | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 3 |
| NCKH giải thưởng khác (`RESEARCH_OTHER`) | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | Không áp dụng |

Điểm giải thưởng và điểm tham gia được cộng nếu người gửi chọn tham gia, kể cả khi đã có giải. Ví dụ Olympic toàn quốc giải Nhất và tham gia được 16 điểm; chỉ tham gia được 6 điểm. Các hồ sơ cộng độc lập, không áp dụng chỉ lấy giải cao nhất hoặc trần 10 điểm. Lựa chọn tham gia ở nhóm chưa có mức điểm bị từ chối; hồ sơ phải có giải hoặc tham gia hợp lệ.

Điểm quốc tế chưa có quy tắc nên chưa hỗ trợ ở form thành tích. Phạm vi `INTERNATIONAL` của bài vinh danh vẫn có thể ghi nhận kỷ niệm và không cộng điểm. Các nhóm hoạt động khác ngoài học thuật được để lại cho đợt sau. Điểm trên nền tảng là tổng theo quy tắc này; không thay thế kết quả đánh giá rèn luyện chính thức của trường.

## Duyệt và quyền riêng tư

- Public profiles require an active, nondeleted identity and return only approved+visible records. The count is records, not prizes; `publicPoints` sums their award+participation points. Rankings are separate: active STUDENT, explicit opt-in, all approved records including private ones, optional achieved-date year. Ordering is defined below; equal scores no longer share ranks. Changing visibility does not redefine confirmed points; opting out removes the row without deleting records.
- The [fresh V1 baseline](../../apps/api/src/main/resources/db/migration/V1__baseline.sql) defaults `public_visible` to true. It replaces the explicitly authorized **unapplied** V1–V24 history; the former one-time visibility backfill has no existing rows on a fresh install and is removed. Only approved records of active identities become anonymously readable; review/score/files/ranking consent do not change. Owners can opt out afterward; restart/migration replay must not republish them. This is not authorization to rewrite existing databases or guess a formerly private set.
- [PublicAchievementResponse](../../apps/api/src/main/java/me/nghlong3004/olympic/recognition/response/PublicAchievementResponse.java) excludes evidence metadata/URLs, submitter/reviewer IDs/notes/times and version. Account DTOs are separate. Evidence is always private, active owner/admin-authorized original bytes with `no-store`/`nosniff`; public achievement/profile visibility confers no evidence access.
- Owners manage their submissions/visibility; admin reviews PENDING, requires reasons for rejection/revocation and uses `expectedVersion`. Stale decisions conflict rather than overwrite; evidence replacement increments version even with unchanged metadata. Album-photo changes likewise version the album. Student/admin multipart metadata+evidence submission is separate from album editing; resubmit replaces evidence.
- PostgreSQL's active-claim uniqueness covers user, case-insensitive title, category and achieved date for PENDING/APPROVED, including concurrent requests. Rejected/revoked history stays stored; resubmission cannot collide with another active claim. No score cap/highest-award-only rule is inferred.

## Ranking order and score-change history

[AchievementRepository.rankings](../../apps/api/src/main/java/me/nghlong3004/olympic/recognition/repository/AchievementRepository.java) assigns unique sequential ranks with `ROW_NUMBER`: approved points descending, **latest attainment of the current total** ascending, then user UUID for deterministic pagination. Returning from32 to16 after revocation establishes a new16-point attainment; a member already at16 ranks ahead. Year totals use the achievement's `achieved_date` year, not the review/event year; all-time and year attainment are independent.

[V3 score events](../../apps/api/src/main/resources/db/migration/V3__recognition_score_events.sql) starts history without inventing legacy timestamps. A known current-total time sorts before an unknown legacy time at the same score; unknown/identical times use UUID only as a technical fallback, never as achievement timing. The additive `scoreTimingKnown` response flag identifies unknown timing without exposing private review timestamps. Visibility/consent/account reactivation do not reset attainment.

[RecognitionServiceImpl.reviewAchievement](../../apps/api/src/main/java/me/nghlong3004/olympic/recognition/service/impl/RecognitionServiceImpl.java) locks student → achievement, flushes the decision and appends a nonzero approval/revocation delta in the same transaction through [RecognitionScoreEventRepository](../../apps/api/src/main/java/me/nghlong3004/olympic/recognition/repository/RecognitionScoreEventRepository.java). No-op reviews/rejections add no event; rollback removes both decision and event. Totals still derive from approved achievements, not an event-maintained balance. Latest event ID selects the student's last serialized change; its database timestamp is recorded after the student lock, not inferred from mutable review dates. It is not a globally ordered commit timestamp across different students.

Apply V3 before the matching API and drain old Recognition writers before enabling the ordering: mixed old/new writers can change totals without recording timing. Keep the additive migration on rollback; an old-writer rollback requires a write freeze and timing reconciliation before re-enabling this rule. No automatic backfill, database reset or live rollout is implied.

## Storage and recovery

The V1 baseline includes albums/photos/achievements/evidence/preferences; API schema precedes matching web use. Keep subsequently applied migrations. Private evidence/photo bytes stay in PostgreSQL behind authorized endpoints, not invented public Cloudinary URLs. Actual validation lives in [RecognitionUploadPolicy](../../apps/api/src/main/java/me/nghlong3004/olympic/recognition/service/RecognitionUploadPolicy.java) and [web validation](../../apps/web/src/features/recognition/validation.ts): evidence1–3 files,5MiB each/15MiB total; album max10 JPEG/PNG/WebP photos,5MiB each/15MiB batch. Ensure request/ingress limits accommodate metadata. Max50 pending achievements/student; log IDs/status/counts, never private bytes or Hibernate JDBC bind values.

[HonorEditor](../../apps/web/src/features/recognition/honor-editor.tsx) saves DRAFT before new-photo upload and publishes only after successful upload. Failure can leave a saved draft; retained id/version/files enable retry while open, closing loses unsent File objects. On409, compare/refetch rather than overwrite. Album save/upload/publish is not one atomic operation. The [controlled evidence picker](../ui/owners/identity-and-recognition.md#selecting-and-submitting-recognition-evidence) never simulates persistence; [private viewer](../ui/owners/dialogs-and-media.md#recognition-depthcarousel-and-evidence-capability) has no Recognition PDF first-page contract and retains original download.
